SpellViewer Addon for World of Warcraft 3.3.5a (WotLK)
======================================================
Version: 1.2.0
Author: Kardinolas

INSTALLATION:
1. Download or copy the addon files into a folder named "SpellViewer".
2. Ensure the folder contains two files: SpellViewer.toc and SpellViewer.lua.
3. Place the "SpellViewer" folder into your WoW 3.3.5a directory: World of Warcraft\Interface\AddOns\
4. Launch World of Warcraft 3.3.5a (Project Ascension), verify "SpellViewer" is enabled in the AddOns list.
5. Log into the game. Click the minimap button or type /sv to open the window.
6. Recording is active automatically on load! When you re-roll, the candidate pool automatically refreshes and saves to your active specialization.
7. Click any class category header to expand or collapse it for faster navigation through large wildcard pools.

SLASH COMMANDS:
/sv - Toggles the SpellViewer player abilities window
/spellviewer - Toggles the SpellViewer player abilities window

FEATURES:
- Draggable minimap button (Left-click to toggle, Right-click/drag to orbit)
- Clean dialog window listing all active player abilities
- Mouse-over GameTooltip showing rank, resource cost, cast time, cooldown & description
- Fully compatible with 3.3.5a (build 12340) private servers & client
